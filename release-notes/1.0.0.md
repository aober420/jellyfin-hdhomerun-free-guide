## ✨ New Features

- **Free TV guide:** Download the two-day HDHomeRun guide without entering an email or buying a subscription.
- **Automatic setup:** Add the XMLTV guide provider to Jellyfin using your configured HDHomeRun tuners.
- **Background updates:** Keep the guide updated roughly once a day with fresh tuner authorization.
- **Additional tuners:** Enter tuner addresses in the plugin settings when needed.
